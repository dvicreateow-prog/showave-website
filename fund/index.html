PROJECT DIRECTION

Decisions follow the work.

Contributors and maintainers shape priorities through discussion, review and testing. Funding may help deliver that work, but it does not buy control of the roadmap.

RECOGNITION

Give credit with consent.

Verified sponsors may be recognized publicly when the arrangement and identity are approved. Anonymous support is welcome. Recognition is not a product endorsement.

COMMUNITY FUNDING

Support, without ownership.

Donations and sponsorship are support for shared development. They do not provide equity, guaranteed financial returns or priority over the contribution review process.
