PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED 20 OCTOBER 2026

YOUR WORK.
YOUR DEVICE.

This policy explains what the SHOWAVE website and the SHOWAVE Live app for Windows collect, where it is kept and how you control it, including the optional connection to your YouTube account.

SHOWAVE · A project by Afro Unity Group

In short.

  • You do not need an account with SHOWAVE. SHOWAVE operates no server that receives your media, Show files, settings or account data.
  • SHOWAVE Live keeps your work, settings and sign-ins on your computer. When you go live, it sends your stream directly to the destinations you choose.
  • Connecting YouTube is optional. SHOWAVE Live uses that permission only to set up, run and end live broadcasts on your own channel.
  • SHOWAVE does not sell or share your data, does not show advertising and does not use your data for advertising.

On this page: SHOWAVE Live and your YouTube account · Other data in SHOWAVE Live · The SHOWAVE website · Changes and contact

SHOWAVE Live and your YouTube account.

SHOWAVE Live uses YouTube API Services. If you choose Sign in for a YouTube destination, your web browser opens Google's own sign-in page, and Google asks you to allow SHOWAVE Live to “Manage your YouTube account” (the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). SHOWAVE never sees your Google password, and it requests no other Google permission.

What SHOWAVE accesses, and why

  • Your channel's basic details. SHOWAVE asks YouTube for the channels on your account and keeps only each channel's ID, name and profile picture link, so you can see which channel is connected and choose it as a destination.
  • Whether live streaming is enabled. SHOWAVE asks YouTube to list at most one of your broadcast IDs and keeps only the result (allowed, or the reason YouTube gives), so it can warn you before Go Live instead of failing partway.
  • YouTube video categories for your region, so you can pick a valid category.
  • The broadcast you start. When you click Go Live, SHOWAVE creates a live broadcast and a live stream on your channel using the details you entered: title, description, category, tags, privacy setting (public, unlisted or private), your made-for-kids declaration, start time and broadcast options such as DVR and embedding. It uploads the thumbnail image you chose, reads back the status of that broadcast and stream, sends your video to the stream address YouTube returns, and ends the broadcast when you stop.
  • Clean-up. If setting up the broadcast fails or you cancel before the stream starts, SHOWAVE deletes the broadcast and stream it has just created, so no empty broadcast is left on your channel.

SHOWAVE does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, playlists, messages, analytics or email address, and it does not create, change or delete anything on your channel apart from the live broadcasts you start from SHOWAVE.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

  • Your YouTube sign-in is an OAuth refresh token and a short-lived access token issued by Google. SHOWAVE Live stores them, together with the permission you granted, the time you connected and your channel's ID, name, profile picture link and live-streaming status, in one file on your computer: %LOCALAPPDATA%\SHOWAVE\Accounts\accounts.bin. The file is encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), which ties it to your Windows user account.
  • Your list of Broadcast destinations is saved on your computer, so it comes back after a restart in both the desktop app and the browser app: %LOCALAPPDATA%\SHOWAVE\Broadcast\channels.json. For each destination it keeps the name (for a connected YouTube channel, the channel's name), whether it is On or Off, its orientation, your made-for-kids choice, and the IDs that link it to your YouTube channel and to the stored sign-in. This file is not encrypted. It never holds sign-in tokens, stream keys or stream addresses; SHOWAVE leaves them out of every saved entry.
  • Next to it are two small working files with no personal details: setup-token, a random code that SHOWAVE's pages must present to change the saved list, and on-air.json, which normally exists only while you stream. It records, by internal ID, which SHOWAVE app is sending which saved destination, so the desktop app and the browser app never send the same channel at once.
  • The titles, descriptions and other stream details you type stay in SHOWAVE Live on your computer while the app is open. They reach YouTube only when you go live.
  • SHOWAVE Live sends every request directly from your computer to Google over HTTPS. SHOWAVE has no server that receives, relays or stores your Google or YouTube data, so nobody at SHOWAVE can see or read it.

How it is used and shared

  • SHOWAVE uses Google user data only to provide the YouTube features described above, when you ask for them in the app.
  • SHOWAVE does not sell Google user data, does not transfer or disclose it to anyone, and does not use it for advertising, for credit or lending decisions, or to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. The only place your YouTube data goes is back to Google, when SHOWAVE carries out an action you asked for.
  • SHOWAVE Live's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy ↗, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

  • SHOWAVE keeps your YouTube sign-in and your saved destination list on your computer until you remove them as described below. If Google expires or revokes the sign-in, SHOWAVE shows that the channel needs to be reconnected; it never signs in again without you.
  • To remove one YouTube account: in SHOWAVE Live's Broadcast workspace, open the connected channel's account actions and choose Disconnect account…. This deletes that account's stored sign-in and channel details from your computer and removes its channels from your saved destination list. The stream titles and settings you typed stay in the app while it is open, so you can reconnect. Disconnecting does not cancel the permission you gave at Google.
  • To remove everything at once: in the Broadcast workspace's channel list, choose Forget saved channels & sign-ins…. For your Windows user, in both the desktop app and the browser app, this deletes the saved destination list, the saved stream keys and every stored sign-in. The option Also cancel SHOWAVE's access at Google (YouTube) is selected by default. SHOWAVE then asks Google to revoke the permission you granted and shows whether Google confirmed it. Nothing on your channel is deleted. Forget is not available while SHOWAVE is streaming.
  • After Forget, the saved list file stays on your computer with no destinations, only the time you chose Forget. A small file, %LOCALAPPDATA%\SHOWAVE\Accounts\forgotten.json, records a random ID and whether you asked Google to revoke access, so that a sign-in still in progress at that moment is not saved.
  • You can also revoke SHOWAVE Live's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions ↗. Do this if you used Disconnect, if you cleared the Google option in Forget, or if Google did not confirm the revocation. After you revoke it, the stored sign-in no longer works.

Google and YouTube terms

By connecting a YouTube account to SHOWAVE Live you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service ↗. The Google Privacy Policy ↗ describes how Google handles your data.

Other data in SHOWAVE Live.

  • Your media, Show files, recordings and settings stay on your computer. SHOWAVE Live also keeps a recovery copy of your open project and your recent-projects list in %APPDATA%\SHOWAVE Live Preview. It does not upload any of this unless you stream or send it to a destination yourself.
  • Stream keys and ingest addresses you paste for other destinations are stored on your computer in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SHOWAVE\Accounts\endpoints.bin, encrypted with Windows Data Protection in the same way, and are not shown back in the interface. Your saved destination list refers to them only by ID. Forget saved channels & sign-ins… deletes them.
  • When you go live, your video and audio travel from your computer directly to each destination you choose, such as YouTube, Twitch or your own RTMP or SRT server. Each destination handles your stream under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • If you pair a phone, tablet or laptop from Preferences › Remote Controller, those devices connect to SHOWAVE Live over your local network.
  • SHOWAVE Live contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service, and it does not check for updates in the background.
  • The app's interface loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts when a window opens, so Google receives your IP address and browser details, as it would from any website that uses Google Fonts.
  • Other services are contacted only when you use the feature that needs them: online fonts (Fontsource and jsDelivr), online icons (Iconify), photo search (Wikimedia Commons), the network speed test (Cloudflare), WHIP output (Google's STUN server) and any web page you add as a widget.

The SHOWAVE website.

  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and preview installers are downloaded from GitHub Releases. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details when you visit or download; see the GitHub Privacy Statement ↗.
  • The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising. Its scripts, fonts and images are served from this site.
  • Some pages save your choices in your browser's local storage: appearance and motion preferences, whether you have seen the welcome, and the drafts you write for contributions, Academy activities and surveys, and merchandise cart choices. They stay in your browser and are not sent to SHOWAVE. You can clear them on those pages or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Writing a contribution draft does not publish anything. Public recognition as a contributor or sponsor requires maintainer verification, and showing your name requires your explicit consent.
  • The Ask SHOWAVE guide answers from this site's own content. Your questions stay in the page and are not sent to an AI service. Choosing a video for Academy or the showreel creates a temporary preview on your device; the file is not uploaded.
  • Support links open Flutterwave's donation checkout, where the current recipient is Novus Foundation Uganda. Anything you enter there, including payment details, goes to Flutterwave under its own privacy policy, not to SHOWAVE.

Changes and contact.

When this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If SHOWAVE Live changes how it uses Google user data, the app will ask for your consent before using the data in the new way.

Questions or complaints about privacy: afro.unity256@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Use.
