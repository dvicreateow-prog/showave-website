PRIVACY / LOCAL PREVIEW DRAFT

YOUR WORK.
YOUR DEVICE.

This is a development notice for the localhost website. A final policy for a published service has not been established here.

SHOWAVE · Powered by Afro Unity Group

Browser drafts and preferences.

The contribution workspace stores draft type, date, description and optional display name in this browser under showave.community.drafts.v1. Drafts are not uploaded or counted as verified public contributions. Anonymous drafts omit the stored name. You can remove drafts or export a JSON copy; clearing browser storage removes the local copy.

The motion preference may be stored under showave.site.motion. Browser storage is optional; the interface reports when draft storage is unavailable.

Public recognition.

The verified contributor and sponsor registry begins empty. Public records require maintainer verification and public evidence; publishing a name also requires explicit consent. Writing a local draft does not publish your identity or grant access to the private development repository.

Downloads and external destinations.

This localhost server serves preview installer files and does not create an account or process payment details. Donation links open Flutterwave, whose checkout currently identifies Novus Foundation Uganda. GitHub and other external destinations operate under their own policies.

The preview has no shared contributor submission backend. Its browser drafts and donation navigation are separate systems. Before a public launch, this notice needs review against the actual hosting, contact, account and community services that will be used.

New local workflows.

Welcome preferences and recent guide visits, Academy activity drafts, merchandise cart choices and survey-response drafts are stored in this browser. You can clear them from their respective pages. Store request notes are included only in your export. The guide assistant keeps questions in the current session and does not send them to an AI service.

Choosing a video for Academy or the showreel creates a temporary preview on this device; its file is not uploaded or stored by the website. An Academy draft can contain the title and HTTPS link you enter, but that link is not automatically embedded. Public posts, shared survey collection, accounts, commerce and AI services require a separate connection before publication.
