PRIVACY / UPDATED 15 OCTOBER 2026

YOUR WORK.
YOUR DEVICE.

This notice explains the public SHOWAVE website and the optional Google/YouTube connection in the Windows desktop preview. SHOWAVE is developed by Afro Unity Group.

Website hosting and downloads.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and GitHub Releases delivers the installers. GitHub processes connection information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under its privacy statement. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics services, or a website account system.

Drafts stay in your browser.

Welcome preferences, recent guides, contribution drafts, Academy activity, merchandise request drafts and survey answers are saved only in your browser when you choose to save them. Drafts are not sent to a shared submission service or counted as public contributions. Export creates a file on your device. You can clear drafts on their pages or remove their browser storage. Questions to Ask SHOWAVE stay in the current session; no AI service receives them.

Videos selected for Academy or showreel review are temporary local previews. The website does not upload those files. Public contribution records require maintainer verification, supporting public evidence, and consent before a person's name is published.

Optional Google and YouTube access in the desktop app.

When you choose Connect YouTube in the Windows app, your system browser opens Google's authorization screen. Each user chooses their own Google account and YouTube channel. SHOWAVE requests YouTube account access to identify your channel, check live-stream eligibility, create and bind a broadcast and stream, upload a chosen thumbnail, and complete a broadcast when you stop it.

The desktop helper receives authorization tokens, channel identifiers and display information, live-broadcast information, and the streaming endpoint/key needed to deliver the programme to YouTube. Google access and refresh tokens and active account connections remain in the current helper session's memory and are cleared when that session closes. The website and the publisher's GitHub repositories do not receive these account tokens or your media. Your programme is sent to your selected streaming destination when you explicitly start output.

Google user data is used only to provide the channel-connection and broadcast features you request. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or send it to AI model training services. SHOWAVE's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and removal.

You can disconnect an account in SHOWAVE or close its session to clear the in-memory connection. You can also revoke SHOWAVE's access in your Google account connections. Revocation does not automatically delete broadcasts or videos already created on YouTube; manage those in YouTube Studio. The Google app currently has External / Testing access, so only listed test users can authorize it.

External links and support.

Optional support links open Flutterwave, whose current recipient is Novus Foundation Uganda. SHOWAVE's website does not collect payment-card details or process a payment. GitHub, Google, YouTube and Flutterwave apply their own policies when you use their services.

Contact.

For privacy questions or requests, contact afro.unity256@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, account tokens or stream keys by email or in issue reports. This notice will be updated when the actual services or data practices change.
