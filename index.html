From your first cue
to the final output.

A practical route through preparation, performance and the audience picture. Start small, rehearse with your own content and learn one production task at a time.
01 / PREPARE
Your first show
WITH
Live + Present

Start with one original image, video, PDF or supported PowerPoint file. Load it into a Clip, wait for the real first picture and rehearse your controls. Prepare a slide in Presenter Preview; Take slide commits it to the pinned feed. Send to Show triggers the pinned Clip.

02 / COMPOSE
Your audience picture
WITH
SHOWAVE Broadcast

Create a blank scene and add the sources your production needs. Arrange the picture in Preview, check the mixer and take it to Programme with Cut or Fade. Taking a scene does not itself start an online stream. Test the devices and streaming destination as a separate rehearsal.

03 / CONNECT
Your creative outputs
WITH
Mapping + Lighting

Explore output mapping, LED planning and slice sequencing with Map, Grid Tool and Chaser. GSL5 is a linked lighting product with its own patch, cues, playback and output controls. Use appropriate fixture profiles and test real equipment; physical lighting output is explicitly armed inside GSL5.

F.A.Q

Straightforward answers, so you can move forward with confidence.
What is SHOWAVE?

SHOWAVE is a developing software family for live visuals, presentations, broadcasting, media preparation, mapping, sequencing and linked lighting workflows. It is powered by Afro Unity Group.

Who is it for?

Live events and conference teams, visual artists, lighting operators, streamers, YouTubers, educators and DJs can explore tools for their production tasks. Available capabilities depend on the workspace and platform.

Which downloads are available?

Find the current Windows preview and the latest Linux and Android platform notes on the Downloads page. No stable, macOS or iOS release is available yet. Android is an offline interface and player review build.

Does downloading require a donation?

No. The support step is optional; you can continue straight to the preview download. The current Flutterwave link identifies Novus Foundation Uganda as the recipient. A dedicated SHOWAVE donation page is planned.

Where are the manuals and development guides?

The manuals cover your first show, Broadcast, the software family and platform notes. Developer pages describe browser effects, selected local helper APIs and the planned MCP service. A public plugin marketplace and stable extension SDK are still ahead.

How can I contribute or sponsor development?

Choose code, design, testing, documentation, translation or community work on Get involved. Individuals and organizations can support the development fund or prepare a sponsorship proposal. Support does not purchase ownership, financial returns or control of the roadmap.

How is SHOWAVE becoming fully open source?

Our commitment is software everyone can use, study, improve and share. The Source and license guide links our current repository, which requires access, and records the remaining public source and license publication steps.

How do I join the community?

Visit the community to find the current development repository, prepare contribution drafts and read the guides. Repository access may be required. A public forum and shared contributor submission service are not configured yet.
