Know the build you are testing.

SHOWAVE currently offers preview packages, with no stable release yet. Read the platform notes and rehearse with your own content, devices and destination before relying on a workflow in a production. A visible workspace does not mean every native integration is available on every platform.

Changing this website or application source does not update an existing installer snapshot. Check the current package notes ↗

Contributions and recognition.

Local contribution drafts stay on your browser and are not automatically submitted. Their export is an unverified work record. Public graph entries and rankings use checked records; named recognition requires explicit consent. Sponsor badges are concepts until arrangements are confirmed.

Optional support.

Support is optional and never required to download a preview. The current Flutterwave donation destination names Novus Foundation Uganda as its recipient. Check the recipient and details on that external checkout before confirming; this localhost website does not process payments.

What remains to be finalized.

Public hosting and service terms, foundation governance, shared community submissions and formal sponsorship arrangements still need their own published details. The current development repository is private and this website does not grant access.

About SHOWAVE ↗ · Foundation initiative ↗ · Local privacy draft ↗

Learning, merchandise and participation.

Academy activities and survey answers saved here are local drafts. They are not public posts or submitted responses. Use original or permitted material, and only enter identity information you want included in an export.

Merchandise designs are concepts. Cart exports are requests, not orders. Prices, stock, product specifications, fulfillment, payment terms and a published order destination require confirmation. No checkout or reservation is made by this preview.

The roadmap describes current previews and planned work without a committed stable-release date. Development support is optional and uses the recipient disclosed on the donation page.
